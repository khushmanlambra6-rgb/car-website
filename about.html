Our story

Mobility with more intention.

Velora Drive was built around a simple idea: renting a car should feel considered, transparent and personal—not transactional.

Company introduction

A modern rental company for people who care about the journey.

Founded in 2012, Velora Drive began with a small premium fleet and a focus on dependable airport and business rentals. Today, the fictional brand operates a curated multi-category fleet across key Indian travel hubs.

We balance the convenience of digital booking with the reassurance of a human handover. Every rate is designed to be understandable, every vehicle is prepared before collection, and every rental is supported by a real service team.
